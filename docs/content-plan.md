## Questions to resolve

- What are the most important visitor tasks? What belongs on the homepage?
- Which legacy pages should return, redirect or be retired?
- Who writes, approves and maintains text, dates and contact information?
- Which photos and videos may be published, and who holds the permissions?
- Which information is public, and which future features require authentication?
- How should recruitment, ticketing and video link to external services?
- What navigation, visual identity, accessibility and mobile requirements should guide design?
